# 2027 Planner 📅

A simple phone planner for 2027.

- One month at a time: swipe, tap the arrows or tap a month name
- Weeks start on Monday, with week numbers
- Malaysia national public holidays highlighted
- Tap any day to add a note. Notes save automatically on your phone.
- Lists of all holidays and all your notes

**Open:** https://laimeitou1126.github.io/planner-2027/

## Install on a phone

- **Android (Chrome):** menu ⋮ → *Add to Home screen* / *Install app*
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*

Once opened, it also works offline.

## Notes

Dates for religious and lunar holidays (Chinese New Year, Hari Raya, Wesak, Deepavali and others) are expected dates. They can shift once the official list is announced. Replacement and state holidays aren't shown. Notes are kept only on the device where you type them.
